Slide 3: Why Single-Variable Analysis Isn't Enough

- Today's RCA looks at one variable at a time (which step, which Equipment
  Change State) - it explains what's out of target, but not always why in
  combination with other factors.

- Real interruption drivers are often compound: a specific machine group +
  reason code + duration class together, not any single factor alone.

- Single-variable ranking (Pareto-style) can miss a combination that's
  small in total time but highly specific, recurring, and fixable.

- We need to look at variables jointly, and confirm statistically which
  combinations are real drivers versus coincidence.
