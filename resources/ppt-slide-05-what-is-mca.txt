Slide 5: What is Multiple Correspondence Analysis (MCA)?

- Extends the same logic to 3+ factors at once (e.g. Machine Group x
  Reason Code x Interruption Duration Class).

- Finds the compound conditions that explain the most variation in
  interruptions - the ones no single-variable RCA view can show.

- Directly delivers on the Context slide's goal: capture all possible
  patterns, not just the ones found one variable at a time.
