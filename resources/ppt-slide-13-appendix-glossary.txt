Slide 13: Appendix - Glossary & Methodology

- MTBI: Mean Time Between Interruptions.
- Cramer's V: strength of association between two variables (0 = none,
  1 = perfect).
- p-value: probability the observed pattern is due to chance
  (< 0.05 = statistically significant).
- Lift: how many times more often a combination occurs versus random
  chance.
- Standardized residual: how far an observed count is from what's
  expected if there were no association.
- Full methodology detail is available in the analysis output
  (INTERPRETATION.md / report.html).
