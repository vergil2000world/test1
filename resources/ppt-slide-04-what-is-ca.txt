Slide 4: What is Correspondence Analysis (CA)?

- A statistical method that tests whether two categorical factors (e.g.
  Machine Group x Reason Code) are truly related to interruptions, or
  just co-occurring by chance.

- Produces a significance test (chi-square, p-value) plus a "map" showing
  which categories cluster together.

- Answers the question "is this pattern real, and how strong is it?" with
  a number (p-value, Cramer's V) instead of a gut feel.
