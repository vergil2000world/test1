Slide 1: Context

- What MTBI is: MTBI (Mean Time Between Interruptions) measures machine
  interruptions across process steps, packages, and lines.

- How we use it today: Root Cause Analysis (RCA) identifies why a step or
  line falls out of MTBI target - based on Equipment Change State - and
  drives the resulting action plan.

- The gap: All this data is collected, but not fully analyzed. We're not
  turning data into information - which is holding back our MTBI maturity.

- Why that matters: RCA alone doesn't capture every pattern. To make the
  analysis process robust, we need to capture all possible patterns, not
  just the ones RCA finds one variable at a time.
