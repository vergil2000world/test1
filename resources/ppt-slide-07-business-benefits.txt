Slide 6: Business Benefits

- Faster root cause: minutes instead of days of manual cross-tabbing
  across steps, lines, and packages.

- Finds hidden, compounding interruption drivers that single-variable
  RCA misses.

- Statistically defensible: every finding comes with a significance
  test, not an opinion.

- Repeatable: re-run every reporting period on new interruption data -
  grows our MTBI maturity instead of being a one-off analysis.

- Prioritizes action: ranks combinations by confidence and impact, so
  the team fixes the highest-payoff root cause first.
