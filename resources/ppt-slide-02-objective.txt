Slide 2: Objective

- Goal: turn the interruption data we already collect into information that
  improves MTBI, not just a one-off RCA case.

- Approach: analyze the full interruption log (Machine, Duration, Reason
  Code, Lot ID, Product Code, Machine Group, Equipment Change State, and
  the counted-interruption flag) using statistical association techniques,
  not just manual spreadsheet review.

- Deliverable: a repeatable, automated process that surfaces every
  significant pattern driving MTBI - not only the ones RCA happens to
  catch one step at a time.
